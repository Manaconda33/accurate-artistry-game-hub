# McFleurdel checkerboard outfit review checklist

Product-owner visual review should verify all eleven candidates against the pre-change approved source package:

- face, makeup, expression, and identity are unchanged;
- hair geometry and view-specific black/white placement are unchanged;
- posture, seated footprint, limbs, and hand positions are unchanged;
- checkerboard blazer treatment reads consistently across front/rear/action states;
- black formal core, white shirt front, bow-tie treatment, and restrained studded waist detail read as one outfit family;
- lower-body checker accents remain secondary to the blazer and do not obscure the kart silhouette;
- hit and victory poses retain their approved silhouette and state readability;
- no kart or steering-wheel pixels were added to any sprite;
- transparent gaps around hair/arms/body remain clean;
- portrait remains recognizable at Character Select scale.

Approval of this candidate package is an asset approval only. Integration/revision update and live publication remain separate gates.
